## 🎓 **How to Use New Features**

### Reports & Analytics
1. Click "Reports" in sidebar
2. Select date range (7d, 30d, 90d, All)
3. Switch between tabs: Sales, Products, Customers, Channels
4. View charts and rankings
5. Analyze business performance

### Returns Management
1. Click "Returns" in sidebar
2. Click "New Return" button
3. Select original order
4. Choose items to return
5. Enter reason and refund amount
6. Track status through workflow

### Global Search
1. Press **Ctrl+K** anywhere
2. Type at least 2 characters
3. View categorized results
4. Click to navigate directly

### CSV Import/Export
1. Go to Products page
2. Click "Export" to download CSV
3. Click "Import" to upload CSV
4. Products added automatically

### Audit Log
1. Go to Settings page
2. Scroll to "Activity Log" section
3. Search or filter activities
4. View all operations history
